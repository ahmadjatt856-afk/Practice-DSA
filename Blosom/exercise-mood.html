❤ Exercise & Mood Support

Phase-based workouts and nutrition guidance

1. Menstrual Phase

Light yoga, stretching, iron-rich foods

2. Follicular Phase

Cardio, strength training, protein

3. Ovulation Phase

Flexibility, fiber, antioxidants

4. Luteal Phase

Pilates, healthy fats, warm foods

5. Lifestyle Tips

Hydration, sleep, mindfulness

Menstrual Phase

Exercises: Light yoga, Stretching, Walking

Foods: Iron-rich foods, Soups, Magnesium

Follicular Phase

Exercises: Cardio, Strength training, High-intensity

Foods: Protein, Eggs, Beans, Complex carbs

Ovulation Phase

Exercises: Maintain strength, Flexibility, Stay hydrated

Foods: Fiber, Fruits, Antioxidants

Luteal Phase

Exercises: Moderate workouts, Pilates, Cycling, Swimming

Foods: Healthy fats, Complex carbs, Warm foods

Lifestyle Tips

  • Stay hydrated (8+ glasses daily)
  • Reduce caffeine and sugar
  • Practice mindfulness and meditation
  • Sleep 7–9 hours
