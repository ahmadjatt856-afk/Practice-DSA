🌿 PCOS Complete Guide

Information and guidance for managing PCOS

What is PCOS?

PCOS is a hormonal imbalance affecting women of reproductive age. It causes irregular periods, acne, weight gain, and mood swings. Manageable with proper diet and exercise.

Common Symptoms

  • Irregular or missed periods
  • Weight gain (belly fat)
  • Acne or oily skin
  • Excess facial hair
  • Hair thinning
  • Mood swings and fatigue

Recommended Diet

  • High-fiber vegetables (Spinach, Broccoli, Kale)
  • Lean proteins (Fish, Chicken)
  • Whole grains (Brown rice, Oats, Quinoa)
  • Avoid: Sugar, white bread, junk food

Exercise Plan

  • Follicular → Cardio + Strength training
  • Ovulation → Flexibility workouts
  • Luteal → Yoga, Pilates, Light walking
  • Menstrual → Stretching, Rest

Helpful Supplements

  • Inositol (insulin balance)
  • Omega-3 (reduces inflammation)
  • Vitamin D (hormonal support)
  • Magnesium (anxiety + sleep)
